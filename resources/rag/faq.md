## What is Pebble?

Pebble is a fictional note-taking and task-management app used to demonstrate this RAG pipeline. It lets individuals and small teams capture notes, organize them into boards, and turn them into actionable tasks.

## How much does Pebble cost?

Pebble offers a free plan for up to 50 notes, a Pro plan at $6/month per user with unlimited notes and boards, and a Team plan at $12/month per user with shared workspaces and admin controls.

## Does Pebble have a mobile app?

Yes. Pebble is available on iOS and Android, and notes sync automatically across all of your devices within a few seconds of being saved.

## Can I use Pebble offline?

Yes. Pebble caches your most recent notes and boards locally, so you can keep reading and writing while offline. Changes sync automatically the next time you connect to the internet.

## How do I invite teammates to a Pebble workspace?

Open any workspace, go to Settings > Members, and enter your teammate's email address. They'll receive an invite link by email that lets them join the workspace immediately after accepting.

## Does Pebble support Markdown?

Yes. Pebble notes support standard Markdown formatting, including headings, bold and italic text, bullet and numbered lists, checkboxes, and code blocks.

## Can I export my notes from Pebble?

Yes. From any board, choose Export, and select Markdown, PDF, or CSV. Exports include all notes, tags, and task statuses in that board.

## How does Pebble handle data privacy?

Pebble encrypts all notes in transit using TLS and at rest using AES-256. Workspace data is never shared with other workspaces, and you can request a full data export or deletion at any time from Settings > Privacy.

## What happens if I cancel my Pebble subscription?

Your workspace reverts to the free plan limits at the end of your current billing period. Notes and boards beyond the free plan's limits become read-only until you either upgrade again or trim your workspace down to fit the free tier.

## Does Pebble integrate with calendar apps?

Yes. Pebble connects with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar. Once connected, tasks with due dates automatically appear as calendar events, and you can create new Pebble tasks directly from a calendar invite.

## Can I set reminders for tasks in Pebble?

Yes. Open any task, click Remind Me, and choose a date and time. Pebble sends a push notification to your mobile app and an email reminder if you have email notifications enabled.

## How do I recover a deleted note in Pebble?

Deleted notes move to the Trash for 30 days before being permanently removed. Open Trash from any workspace's sidebar, find the note, and click Restore to bring it back to its original board.
